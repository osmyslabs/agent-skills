# Security reporting

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/osmyslabs/agent-skills/security/advisories/new).
Do not post credentials, private source, customer material or sensitive traces
in public issues. The maintainer is [@osmyslabs](https://github.com/osmyslabs).

The current published experimental release is the supported review target.
Support and response times are best effort. No stable support window or
production-security certification is promised.

Skills are instructions and may include executable helpers. Review the package
and pin a trusted revision before use. The helpers here perform local copying,
fixture preparation and inspection; they do not need provider credentials or
grant permissions beyond the agent host's existing rules.
